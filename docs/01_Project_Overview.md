## Customer Churn Prediction Pipeline ##


Customer churn is a very often happening events in any of the companies. This churn impact the revenue somewhere of the company. The customer attrition impact the company in various forms like customer acquisition cost increases, the advertisement pricing to gain new customer etc etc. If it is known or predicted that a customer want to leave or is leaving, the company could make them stay by giving them offers, dicounts which will cost less than advertising.

The main objectives of this projects are:
1. To find the patterm of customers leaving (if any)
2. To create a ML model using existing record to predict the customers action to provide them offers or help.

By the help of this model, a company could segregate the customer and could make them stay.

Project Scope:
This project will
1. analyze historical customer data,
2. identify factors related to churn,
3. train and evaluate ML models,
4. provide churn predictions through a user interface.

This project will not
1. automatically contact customers,
2. access real-time company databases,
3. replace human business decisions.
